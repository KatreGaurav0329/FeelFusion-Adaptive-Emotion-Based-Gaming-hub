Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 23, 2025

Your privacy is fundamentally important to us. This policy outlines how FeelFusion handles your information.

1. Data Collection and Usage: The Webcam

FeelFusion requires access to your webcam to enable its core feature: emotion-adaptive gameplay. When you grant permission, our application analyzes the video stream from your camera.

2. Our Core Privacy Promise: 100% Local Processing

We do not collect, send, store, or record any video, images, or personal data from your webcam. All facial detection and emotion analysis happens exclusively within your web browser on your own device. The data never leaves your computer. Once you close the browser tab, the connection to the webcam is terminated and all analysis ceases.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We use your browser's `localStorage` for one purpose only: to save your theme preference (Light Mode or Dark Mode). This is a simple setting that contains no personal information and is stored on your device so the site remembers your choice on your next visit.

4. Third-Party Services

This website uses `particles.js`, a JavaScript library loaded from a third-party Content Delivery Network (CDN) for visual effects. This third party may have its own privacy policy and data collection practices, which we do not control.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy in the future. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
