Our Technology & Mission

What is FeelFusion?

FeelFusion is more than just a gaming website; it's an experiment in empathetic technology. We believe that games can be more immersive and personal when they respond not just to our clicks and keystrokes, but to our genuine emotions. Our platform serves as a showcase for this vision, offering classic arcade games reimagined with an emotional twist.

How The Magic Works

The core of FeelFusion is a powerful, browser-based machine learning library called face-api.js, which runs on the TensorFlow.js engine. Here’s a simple breakdown of the process:

  1. Webcam Access: With your permission, the website accesses your webcam feed.
  2. Local Analysis: Our script analyzes the video feed in real-time, directly within your browser. It identifies key facial landmarks to detect expressions like happiness, surprise, or a neutral state.
  3. Privacy is Paramount: This is the most important step. The video feed is never sent to a server, recorded, or stored. All the "magic" happens and stays on your computer.
  4. Adaptive Gameplay: The detected emotion is translated into a gameplay variable. A smile might increase the speed in *Whack-a-Mole*, while a focused, neutral expression might make obstacles in *Flappy Bird* more challenging.

Our Mission

Our goal is to explore the future of interactive entertainment. We aim to create experiences that are more intuitive, accessible, and emotionally resonant. We hope FeelFusion inspires both players and developers to think differently about the connection between humans and technology.
